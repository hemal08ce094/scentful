Scentful

Fragrance wardrobe for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch · App Store · updated 21 Nov 2026

What is new in 1.1

Scentful keeps the fragrances a person owns, wants and wears. Version 1.1 rebuilds the app around three things.

  • The wardrobe. Scan a bottle label to add it. Each bottle has a status (In rotation, For sale, Finished, Sold, Gifted), a fill level, a price and backups. The Scent Diary logs each wear with occasion, weather and a rating, and a Numbers page shows cost per wear, bottles not worn in 60 days and a shareable year card.
  • Today's Pick and the Concierge. One bottle from the wardrobe each morning, chosen by time of day, season (by hemisphere) and recent wears. The Concierge answers a mood with five picks, written by the on-device Apple Intelligence model. Apple Watch shows Today's Pick and logs the wear with one tap, and a complication carries it on the watch face.
  • iPhone Duo. Unfolded, the catalog sits on one side of the fold and the bottle on the other. Half open on a table, the top half keeps the app and the bottom half shows Today's Pick and the diary entry.
