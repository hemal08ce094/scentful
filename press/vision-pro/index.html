Scentful on Apple Vision Pro

Fragrance wardrobe · App Store · iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch press kit · updated 22 Nov 2026

What is new

Scentful keeps the fragrances a person owns, wants and wears. Version 1.1 adds a native visionOS app to the same App Store record. It has a window with the collection, the wishlist, the catalog of 525 fragrances and Today's Pick, and a second space called the lounge.

In the lounge, the bottles a person owns stand on a curved walnut vitrine in a lamplit room at dusk. Each bottle is built in RealityKit with the juice at the fill level the person recorded, and its label shows the house and the name. Look at a bottle and pinch to open its card: accords, top, heart and base notes, and the person's own fill, purchase date, price, wears and cost per wear, with Log a wear, Wishlist and Open in window. Pinch and drag picks a bottle up and turns it in the hand; letting go over the marble tray sets it down there, and letting go anywhere else returns it to its shelf. Pinching and holding the cap plays a short spritz and logs nothing. Today's Pick stands on the tray when the lounge opens. The Digital Crown sets how much of the room surrounds the person.

Facts

Version
1.1 (build 100) for visionOS, built with Xcode 27.x, submitted to App Review 22 Nov 2026
Platform
Native visionOS app (not iPad compatibility), window plus a progressive immersive space
Languages
English (US, UK, Australia, Canada) and Spanish (Mexico)
Catalog
525 fragrances, bundled with the app. Users can add bottles from the catalog on Vision Pro or import a Scentful backup file.
Price
USD 0.99, paid once, and the same purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Vision Pro. No in-app purchases, no ads, no account.
Privacy
No server. The collection stays on the device, or in the person's private iCloud database when signed in. iCloud is optional.
Accessibility
Reduce Motion turns the lift and glide animations into instant moves. Bottles in the window read their name, house and fill to VoiceOver. The room does not move and there is no locomotion.
Room assets
Interior photograph, textures and spritz sound are CC0 (Poly Haven, ambientCG, Freesound); bottles, labels and furniture are generated in the app.
