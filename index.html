Scentful

Your fragrance wardrobe, kept on your phone.

Scentful keeps track of the bottles you own, the ones you want, and what you wore on which day. It suggests a bottle for today, helps you find something for a mood, and works without an account.

Independent. Scentful isn't affiliated with any fragrance website, retailer or perfume house. It has no reviews feed and no sign-in. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What's inside

  • A catalog of 525 fragrances from 93 houses, plus any fragrance you add yourself
  • Wardrobe, wishlist, shelves and a scent diary
  • Today's Pick and the Scent Concierge, run on device with Apple Intelligence where available
  • Label scanning, wear stats, cost per wear, and JSON or CSV export
  • Apple Watch app for logging what you're wearing
