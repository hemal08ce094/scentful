Support

Write to hemalmodi3@gmail.com. In the app, More > Send feedback adds your app version for you.

A fragrance I own isn't in the catalog

Search for it, then tap Add under "Not in the catalog?". You can also tap + in Catalog, Wardrobe or Wishlist, or scan the bottle's label. Fragrances you add work everywhere in the app, including the Concierge and the Watch.

Can I sign in with my account from a fragrance website?

No. Scentful is independent and has no accounts. It doesn't show reviews from other sites.

How do I move my collection to a new phone?

Go to More > Your data > Back up everything, and save the file. On the new phone, open the same screen and choose Restore a Scentful backup. You can also import a CSV with name and house columns.

The Concierge says Apple Intelligence is off

The Concierge uses Apple Intelligence when your device supports it and it's turned on in Settings. Without it, Scentful still makes suggestions by matching accords.
