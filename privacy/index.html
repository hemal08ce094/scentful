Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Scentful doesn't collect any data. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server. We can't see anything you put into it.

What stays on your device

Your wardrobe, wishlist, shelves, diary entries, notes, prices, bottle photos and the fragrances you add are stored on your iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch. Deleting the app deletes them. The Apple Watch app syncs with your iPhone directly, through Apple's WatchConnectivity, and not through us.

Apple Intelligence

The Scent Concierge and Today's Pick use Apple's on-device language model when your device supports it. Your requests are processed on the device and aren't sent to us.

Camera and photos

Scentful asks for camera access only when you scan a bottle label. The text is read on your device to find the fragrance. Photos you choose for a bottle are saved inside the app on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Exports and backups

When you export a backup or a spreadsheet, the file goes only where you choose to send or save it.

Feedback

If you use Send feedback, your mail app opens a draft that you can review before sending. We use the email you send only to reply to you.

Children

Scentful doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: hemalmodi3@gmail.com.
